# Restaurant OS

AI-powered, offline-first Restaurant Operating System.

## Vision

Restaurant OS is a Windows-first restaurant management platform designed to provide:

- Point of Sale
- Table Management
- Kitchen Display System
- Kitchen Order Tickets
- Menu Management
- Inventory Management
- Recipe Management
- Purchasing
- Supplier Management
- Customer Management
- Loyalty
- Employee Management
- Expense Management
- Advanced Analytics
- Multi-branch Management
- QR Ordering
- Delivery Management
- AI Restaurant Copilot
- AI Demand Forecasting
- AI Inventory Intelligence
- AI Menu Intelligence
